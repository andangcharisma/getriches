Published on **October 11, 2026**, v0.5.0 is a polish release. Nothing flashy, but a lot of the little things that make an app feel finished: it now starts empty and ready for *your* money, the buttons stop flashing at you, and Android phones finally get the treatment they deserve.

## A fresh start that's actually fresh

Until now, the first thing a new user saw was somebody else's data: four wallets, a month of transactions and a few budgets we'd put there as examples. That made sense when the app was young. It doesn't any more.

Install RICHES today and you start with **nothing but your own blank page**:

- The Dashboard shows `Rp0`, with a friendly "No wallets yet" card and an **Add Wallet** button to get you going.
- Your first wallet is a couple of taps away, and the Add Transaction screen tells you what to do if you don't have a wallet yet.
- Zero amounts now read `Rp0` instead of `+Rp0`. Small thing, but it looked odd.

You don't start with an empty list of categories, though. RICHES comes with a set of **starter categories** so you can log your first expense right away: food and drinks, transport, housing, shopping, health, entertainment, education, family and gifts, bills and fees on the spending side, and salary, business, investment and gifts for income, many with handy subcategories. They're ordinary categories, so rename, remove or add your own whenever you like.

If you already use RICHES, **nothing changes for you**. Your wallets, transactions and categories stay exactly as they are.

## Calmer buttons

Remember that colour that spread out from your finger every time you tapped something? It's gone.

Buttons now respond the way our design intended: a main button gives a tiny **press down** when you tap it, and softer buttons simply tint while you hold them. The menu at the top of Transactions has a neat rounded highlight that matches its shape. And rows in your lists, like transactions or Export Data and Import Data, no longer flash anything when you touch them. It's quieter, and it feels more like the rest of the app.

## Made to fit Android

A few things on Android needed love, and they got it:

- **The bottom menu is no longer hidden.** On phones with the classic three-button navigation, the system bar used to cover the lower part of RICHES' menu, hiding the labels. The menu now sits neatly above it. With gesture navigation it stays right where it was, and it follows along if you switch between the two.
- **The app has its proper name.** After installing, it shows up as **Riches**, not "riches".

## Staying upright

RICHES is designed for holding your phone upright, and now it stays that way. Turn your phone on its side and the app no longer tries to stretch itself across the screen.

## Smaller improvements

- **A tidier Dashboard.** "My Wallets" now shows up to **four wallets**, so it never pushes your recent transactions way down the page. Your total balance still adds up all your wallets, and the full list is one tap away under "See all".
- **Adjust Balance asks you to choose.** If you haven't picked a wallet to work with, nothing is selected for you any more, and Save waits until you've chosen one, so you can't adjust the wrong wallet by accident.
- **A cleaner Wallets screen.** Wallets you've left out of your total no longer wear an "Excluded" label. The heading they sit under already says it, and they're now just as bright as the rest.
- **A new icon for currency.** Your currency setting in Profile now has a little banknote instead of a sun.

## What's next

We'll keep sanding down the rough edges, and we're always glad to hear what you'd like to see next. If something doesn't feel right on your phone, tell us. Thanks for reading, and happy tracking!
