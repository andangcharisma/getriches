Two weeks ago we shared the first version of RICHES. Since then we've been listening to our own use of the app, and a few things kept coming up: "Why is my savings account counted in my total?", "I want to be more specific than just *Food*", and "Where's my profile?"

**v0.2.0**, published on **September 20, 2026**, answers all three. Here's what's new.

## Leave some wallets out of your total

Not every wallet should count towards "how much money do I have". Maybe it's an emergency fund you never touch, or a wallet you only keep for tracking.

Now you can mark any wallet as **excluded from total balance**. It stays in the app with its own balance, but it no longer counts towards the total on your Dashboard and Wallets screen. The Wallets list also groups your wallets into **included** and **excluded**, so it's clear at a glance what's part of your total and what isn't.

## Subcategories: be as specific as you like

"Food" is a big word. Was it groceries, a coffee, or dinner out?

When you add or edit a transaction, you can now pick a **subcategory** under a category. It shows up as "Category · Subcategory" so your history tells you more at a glance.

## Choose the categories each wallet uses

Your cash wallet and your credit card probably don't need the same list of categories. In v0.2.0, **each wallet has its own set of categories**. Pick only the ones that make sense for that wallet, and leave the rest out of the way.

We've also built the groundwork for a dedicated **Categories** screen, covering income, expense and transfer categories with subcategories. You'll see more of it in the next release.

## A new way to browse your months

The month selector on the Transactions screen used to show just three months. It's now a **scrollable strip of the last 12 months** that starts on the current month, so looking back at last spring takes a swipe instead of guesswork.

## Meet the Profile screen

The Profile tab is no longer an empty placeholder. It now has your identity at the top, a Preferences section (language and currency), an Account section, and Log Out.

## A calmer Dashboard

We removed the "This Month's Budget" section from the Dashboard. It was taking up room without helping you answer the main question: *where do I stand today?* The Dashboard now puts your balance, your wallets and your latest transactions front and center.

## Little things that add up

- The **active tab** in the bottom menu now glows in the accent colour, so you always know where you are.
- The center **+ button** sits a little lower, matching the design more closely.
- Secondary text (like greetings and account labels) uses a softer tint across the app, so it's easier on the eyes without losing contrast.

## What's next

v0.3.0 is already underway with a brand-new app icon, a frosted-glass bottom menu, and the ability to back up and restore your data. We'll tell you all about it soon.

As always, if you have thoughts, ideas or bugs for us, we'd love to hear them. Thanks for reading, and happy tracking!
