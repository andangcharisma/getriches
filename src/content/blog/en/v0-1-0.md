Most finance apps want something from you first: an account, an email, a password, sometimes a subscription. But all most people want is to write down where their money goes. So we decided to build something simpler.

Say hello to **RICHES**, a personal finance tracker for iPhone and Android. This is **v0.1.0**, the very first release. It's small on purpose, but it already does what matters most: it shows you, at a glance, where you stand.

## No account. No cloud. Just your phone.

There's no sign-up and no login. Everything you enter stays on your own phone and never leaves it. Open the app and start tracking, that's it. (Syncing across devices is something we'd like to explore later.)

## What's in v0.1.0

### Dashboard: your money at a glance
The home screen is the heart of the app:

- Your **total balance** across all wallets, right on top in a big violet card
- **This month's income and expenses**, so you know how the month is going
- Every **wallet and its balance** (cash, bank, e-wallet, whatever you use), laid out neatly with no sideways scrolling
- Your **latest transactions**, so you can see what just happened
- A **budget section** with progress bars that turn red when you overspend

### Transactions: log it, find it, fix it
- Record **income**, **expenses** and **transfers** between wallets
- Filter by type, by **month** or by **wallet**
- Everything is **grouped by date**, so the list reads like a diary
- Made a mistake? Tap a transaction to edit or delete it. No "are you sure?" pop-ups and no success messages getting in your way.

### Wallets: all your accounts in one place
Create a wallet for your cash, each bank account and each e-wallet, and edit or delete them whenever you like. Balances are **calculated automatically from your transactions**, so you never have to adjust them by hand and they always match your history.

## The look and feel

RICHES has a dark violet theme with a violet-to-pink gradient. We didn't want a spreadsheet in an app costume, so we made it feel good to open:

- Clean, friendly lettering that looks the same no matter what, even without internet
- A bottom menu that **shrinks as you scroll down** and comes back when you scroll up, giving your content more room
- A big **+** button in the middle that takes you straight to adding a new transaction

## What's not here yet

We'd rather be upfront than overpromise. v0.1.0 doesn't have:

- The **Profile** screen (it's a "coming soon" placeholder)
- A way to manage your own categories and budgets
- Backing up or moving your data
- A full Indonesian translation. The design supports both English and Indonesian, but English comes first.

## What's next

v0.2.0 is already in the works, with categories and subcategories, smarter wallet grouping, and a lot of polish. We'll share more soon.

If you try RICHES, we'd love to hear what you think. Thanks for reading, and happy tracking!
