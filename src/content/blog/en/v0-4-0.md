Published on **October 9, 2026**, v0.4.0 is all about the small things that make an app feel like it knows you: it remembers which wallet you use, says the right hello for the time of day, and finally lets you correct a balance that doesn't match your real money. It also adds a few safety nets, so a wrong tap costs you less.

## It remembers your wallet

Most of us log spending from the same wallet again and again. Now RICHES does too.

Pick a wallet in the Transactions filter, and that wallet becomes your **active wallet**. Add a new transaction and the Wallet field is already filled in. Change it for one transaction and nothing else is affected. Close the app completely, open it tomorrow, and it's still there. Choose "All Wallets" to go back to starting with an empty field.

If you delete a wallet that was active, RICHES simply goes back to "All Wallets". No errors, no surprises.

## A hello that fits the hour

The greeting at the top of the Dashboard used to say "Good morning" no matter what. It now follows your clock: **Good morning** until noon, **Good afternoon** until 6 pm, and **Good evening** after that. Leave the app in the background overnight and it will have updated by the time you're back.

## Adjust Balance: make the app match your real money

Sometimes the number in RICHES drifts away from the number in your real wallet or bank. A forgotten coffee, a small fee, a rounding difference. Until now there was no clean way to fix it.

Tap the **⋮ button** at the top right of the Transactions screen and choose **Adjust Balance**:

1. Pick the wallet.
2. Type the balance it should actually have.
3. RICHES shows the difference, in green if your balance goes up and red if it goes down.
4. Save.

RICHES records the difference as a normal transaction, labelled **Adjust Balance** with a ⚖️ icon, so your history stays honest about what happened. You can edit or delete it later like any other transaction.

The Save button stays faded until there is something to adjust. If the balance you type matches the current one, there's nothing to save.

## Two built-in categories you can rely on

Transfers and balance adjustments now have their own categories, **Transfer** and **Adjust Balance**, built into the app. They show up in your category list with a small **padlock**, always selected, and they can't be edited or deleted. That means a transfer always has a proper home, and a balance adjustment never ends up in a category you've renamed or removed.

They also survive **Reset Data**: after a reset you start from a clean slate, but these built-in categories are still there.

If you're updating from an earlier version, **your data stays exactly as it was**. Your existing transfers are simply filed under the new Transfer category.

## Safety nets

- **Import now asks first.** Importing a backup replaces everything currently in the app, so RICHES now asks you to confirm before it even opens your files. It also reminds you to export a backup first if you want one.
- **Reset says what it does.** The wording is simpler: "This deletes all your data. This cannot be undone."
- **Backups from older versions still work.** A backup you made with v0.3.0 can be imported into v0.4.0.
- **Save waits until you're ready.** On Add Wallet, Save stays faded until the wallet has a name. On Add Transaction, it stays faded until there is an amount, a wallet and, for income and expenses, a category. No more error messages after the fact: the button simply tells you it's not ready yet.

## Categories that behave the way you'd expect

When you choose which categories a wallet uses:

- Choosing a **subcategory** automatically chooses its parent category.
- Turning off a **category** turns off all of its subcategories too.
- Turning a category on doesn't switch its subcategories on. You pick those yourself.
- Categories you haven't chosen are no longer dimmed, so everything stays easy to read.

## Little touches

- **Cleaner icons.** The edit button now uses a plain pencil, and the back buttons on the category and wallet screens use a chevron instead of an X.
- **Month tabs that stay put.** On Transactions, the current month now sits right in the middle of the screen, and tapping a month that's half hidden at the edge slides it neatly into view.
- **A search button is on its way.** You'll notice a dimmed magnifier next to the ⋮ button. Search isn't ready yet, but that's where it will live.
