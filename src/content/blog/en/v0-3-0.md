Published on **October 2, 2026**, v0.3.0 is our biggest update so far. Your app gets a fresh face, you get far more say over how your spending is organised, and for the first time your data is truly yours to take with you.

## A brand-new icon

Say goodbye to the old trend-line symbol. RICHES now wears a **gold coin with a rocket** on our violet-to-pink gradient, a nod to the whole point of the app: watching your money take off. You'll spot it on your home screen the moment you update.

## Your categories, your way

Until now categories were something you could pick. In v0.3.0 you can **shape them**.

From the category picker you can now:

- **Add** new categories and subcategories on the spot
- **Edit** a category's name and icon, or **move** it under a different parent
- **Delete** categories you no longer need

It all happens in one place, right where you're already choosing categories. There's no separate screen to hunt for and no extra taps: tap **+** and the form simply appears.

### What happens to your old transactions?

We thought hard about this one, because deleting a category shouldn't wreck your history:

- **Subcategories are kept.** If you delete a category, its subcategories don't disappear. They move up and become categories of their own.
- **Transactions stay put.** Everything you recorded under a deleted category is still there. It's simply labelled **"Uncategorized"**, so you can tell it apart from a transfer between your own wallets.
- Any budget or wallet selection that pointed at the deleted category is tidied up automatically.

## Back up, restore, or start fresh

The Profile screen has a new **Data** section with three options:

- **Export Data** saves all your wallets, categories, transactions and budgets into a single backup file you can share or store anywhere you like.
- **Import Data** restores everything from a backup file. That's handy when you switch phones. If the file isn't a valid RICHES backup, the app says so and leaves your current data untouched.
- **Reset Data** wipes everything so you can start from scratch. Because this one can't be undone, it's the **one place in the app where we ask "are you sure?"** first.

## Small touches you'll feel

- **Tap feedback:** the buttons in the bottom menu now shrink slightly when you press them, so every tap feels acknowledged. Navigation is still instant.
- **Frosted glass:** the bottom menu now has a translucent, blurred look. When you scroll, your content glides softly underneath it.
- **Version number:** the Profile screen now shows which version of RICHES you're running, which is handy when you want to tell us about a problem.
